English

Sea Fishing is a free hobby app for sea anglers on the Adriatic and Ionian coasts. It has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases. We do not sell or share personal data.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location to show the weather and sea conditions where you are and to suggest the nearest area. The location is used on your device. To get a forecast, the app sends the coordinates of the selected point (not your identity) to the weather and map services listed below. You can refuse location access and pick a point on the map instead.

Fishing diary and photos

Diary entries and catch photos are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded. A backup file (zip) or table (CSV) is created only when you export it yourself.

Anonymous usage statistics

The app and the website use Google Analytics for Firebase to count how many people use them, which screens are opened, the app language, the device model, the Android version and the approximate country. This data is not linked to your name and is not used for advertising. Advertising ID collection is turned off. You can switch statistics off at any time in Settings → “Anonymous statistics”. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Services the app connects to

  • Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com, marine-api.open-meteo.com): weather, waves, water temperature. Receives the coordinates of the chosen point.
  • EMODnet Bathymetry: sea depth for the chosen point and the depth map.
  • Esri ArcGIS Online: map and satellite images for the visible map area.
  • OpenStreetMap Overpass API: names of towns, islands, capes and bays in the visible map area.
  • NOAA Aviation Weather (aviationweather.gov): wind measured at nearby airports.
  • Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons: fish pictures and articles in the fish library.
  • GitHub Pages: the website itself, and in the version downloaded from the website, the check for new versions.

Like any website, these services see your IP address when the app requests data from them.

Feedback

If you write to us by e-mail or Telegram, we see only what you send and use it only to answer you and improve the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: kind2908k@gmail.com, Telegram @tamtam2908. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your device.
