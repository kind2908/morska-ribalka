English

Sea Fishing is a free hobby app and website for sea anglers in Montenegro, Croatia, Italy, Greece, Albania, Slovenia, Spain (without the Canary Islands) and Turkey. It has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases. We do not sell or share personal data. The app is made by a private developer; contact details are at the end of this page.

What stays on your device

Your fishing diary, catch photos, saved places and settings are stored only on your device: in the app's storage on Android, or in your browser's storage on the website. They are never uploaded. We have no server that stores user data, so we cannot see, change or delete it.

Location

Location access is optional. If you allow it, the app or website uses your location to show the forecast where you are and to suggest the nearest area. To get the forecast, only the coordinates of the selected point are sent to the weather and map services listed below, never to us and never together with your name. You can refuse access and pick a point on the map instead.

Anonymous statistics

Statistics help us see whether the app is used at all. They are on by default and can be switched off at any time in Settings → “Anonymous statistics”.

  • Android app: Google Analytics for Firebase. It sends automatic events (first launch, sessions, updates) with a random installation ID, the app version, the app language, the device model, the Android version and the approximate country. The advertising ID is removed from the app, ad personalisation is off.
  • Website: Google Analytics 4 (the same Firebase project). It sends a random visitor ID (stored in a cookie), which tab is opened (forecast, conditions, days, map, diary, fish, settings), the site version, the APK download and the home-screen install, the language and the approximate country. IP anonymisation is on; Google signals and ad personalisation are off. When statistics are off, the Google script is not loaded.

The diary, photos, saved places and coordinates are never part of the statistics. We see only summary reports and cannot link them to a person. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing how many people use the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); you object by switching statistics off. The data is kept by Google under the retention settings of Google Analytics: policies.google.com/privacy.

Services the app and website connect to

  • Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com, marine-api.open-meteo.com): weather, waves, water temperature, currents. Receives the coordinates of the selected point.
  • EMODnet Bathymetry: sea depth at the selected point and the depth map.
  • Esri ArcGIS Online: map and satellite images for the visible map area.
  • OpenStreetMap Overpass API (app): names of towns, islands, capes and bays in the visible map area.
  • Weather stations (METAR): NOAA Aviation Weather, aviationweather.gov (app), and MET Norway, api.met.no (website).
  • Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons: fish pictures and articles in the fish library.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) (website): the Leaflet map library.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website; the Android version downloaded from the website checks it for new versions. GitHub may keep its own technical logs.

Like any website, these services see your IP address when data is requested from them.

Feedback

If you write to us by e-mail or Telegram, we see only what you send. The feedback form adds the app version, language, country and area, and you see the whole text before sending. We use it only to answer you and improve the app, and delete it on request.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, export, correct and delete your data. Because your data stays on your device, you can do this yourself:

  • Export: Diary → “Backup” (a zip file with entries, photos and places in the app, a JSON file on the website) and “Export diary” (a CSV table).
  • Delete: delete entries in the diary, clear the app's data or the site's data in the browser, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling deletes everything, so make a backup first if you want to keep the diary.
  • Statistics: switch them off in Settings. If you want Analytics data deleted or have any question, write to us. You may also complain to the data protection authority of your country.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
